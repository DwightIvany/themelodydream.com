Dwight Ivany

Dwight is a music sculptor.

For well over 30 years, Dwight Ivany has been recording, producing and engineering beautiful music. His education and skill in electronic engineering means he's mastered the technical realm in virtually any setting, which frees the musician to produce exceptional music. His welcoming personality evokes the best performance from the heart of the artist, where he mixes it with technical magic, to sculpt music that enriches. As he says, "I know an excellent session is much more about setting the right mood than setting up the equipment."

This accomplished producer has worked with over 50 artists, such as C.R. Avery, Mark Berube and Katie Ormiston to produce quality CDs, television scores, and radio productions.
