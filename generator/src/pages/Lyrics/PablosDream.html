Pablo’s Dream

Jazzadelic yellow suit
blows reed through oak
heavy hand of paint
splash notes on a page

There’s a dog in corner
I was wishing that was me
playing jazz for Adam and Eve
jazz for Adam and Eve
jazz for Adam and Eve
the day they were freed

Gratify intention
the lovers dream
lay a castle on the cloud
for Pablo to see
no one lives forever
but a dream never dies
as jazzadelic heros
blow music through the night
oh Pablo’s dream
